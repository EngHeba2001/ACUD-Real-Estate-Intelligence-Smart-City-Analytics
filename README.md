Developed an integrated Real Estate Intelligence and Smart City Analytics platform designed to support data-driven decision-making for the New Administrative Capital (NAC). The platform combines real estate market analytics, business intelligence, predictive modeling, and AI-powered analysis to provide a comprehensive view of real estate performance and urban development.

It analyzes key business indicators, including property sales, collections, unsold inventory, construction progress, and project performance. It also applies predictive analytics and anomaly detection techniques to identify unusual business patterns, forecast future trends, and highlight potential investment and operational risks.

Additionally, the platform incorporates Smart City analytics to evaluate district-level service accessibility, population indicators, and residents' satisfaction, helping identify potential service gaps and areas requiring further investigation.

An AI-powered assistant enables users to ask business-related questions and receive insights supported by available data, while specialized analytical components support investment assessment, developer performance evaluation, and unsold inventory diagnostics.

The main objective is to transform complex real estate and urban data into actionable insights that improve operational efficiency, support investment decisions, and enable smarter urban planning.
